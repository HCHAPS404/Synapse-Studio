# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free environment for everyone.

- Be kind and constructive. Assume good intent.
- No harassment, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Respect others' time: keep discussion focused and technical.
- Credit others' work and follow the licenses of anything you reuse.

Report concerns privately through the repository's private reporting channel.
Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation that violates these
expectations.
